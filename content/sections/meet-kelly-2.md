Kelly’s professional background spans both the private and public sectors. As a small business owner, she has founded, operated, and managed multiple enterprises—experience that gives her practical insight into the needs of entrepreneurs, property owners, and working families. She is currently the executive assistant to the president of J Alan Kent Development, supporting contract acquisitions and major operations.  She is also the owner of KPDC, LLC, providing creative opportunities for adults with special needs.  She previously owned a dance studio and an oilfield consulting company, demonstrating leadership and adaptability across diverse industries. In her public-sector role, Kelly has been instrumental in connecting residents with vital resources, advocating for their needs, and ensuring they receive responsive, effective assistance from the Capitol.

A lifelong Texan, Kelly holds a **Bachelor of Science degree in Gerontology** and a **Master of Education**, both from the University of North Texas. Her academic training provides her with a strong foundation in issues affecting seniors, families, caregivers, and individuals with special needs—perspectives that further strengthen her commitment to thoughtful, community-centered leadership.

At the heart of Kelly’s life are faith, family, and service. She has proudly raised two sons in Spring and remains deeply dedicated to protecting, strengthening, and uplifting the community she calls home. Kelly’s candidacy is grounded in lived experience, long-standing relationships, and a sincere desire to build a stronger, safer, and more prosperous future for Texas House District 126.
