## Issues

### Strong, Conservative Leadership for Texas

1. **Property Tax Relief**  
   Advance meaningful, long-term reforms that _deliver real property tax reductions_ and move Texas toward ultimately _phasing out the property tax burden_ on homeowners.
2. **Flood Mitigation & Community Safety**  
   Strengthen and modernize local infrastructure to _protect homes, reduce flood risk, and keep neighborhoods safe_, while ensuring law enforcement and first responders have the resources they need.
3. **Secure Borders**  
   Support strong, comprehensive measures to _secure the border, remove individuals in the country illegally, and disrupt the operations of dangerous drug cartels._
4. **Economic Growth & Local Jobs**  
   Champion policies that _bolster small businesses, attract new employers, and expand economic opportunity_ for families across the district.
5. **Education Opportunities**  
   Ensure every child has access to a _high-quality education_, support _competitive teacher pay_, and _empower parents_ to make the best decisions for their children. Strengthen and expand _Career and Technical Education (CTE)_ programs—to give students practical pathways to high-demand careers. Kelly is committed to building a strong _pipeline between local CTE programs and the businesses and industries within the district_, ensuring that students graduate career-ready while keeping jobs, talent, and economic growth right here at home.
6. **Defend the Second Amendment**  
   Protect and uphold the _constitutional right to keep and bear arms_, ensuring law-abiding Texans can exercise their freedoms without government overreach.
7. **Sanctity of Life**  
   Stand firm for _pro-life principles_ and advance policies that protect and defend the _most vulnerable among us_.
8. **Reform FM 1960.**  
   FM 1960 in northwest Harris County is plagued by severe congestion, dangerous conditions, and poor planning from unchecked development. We must _reform infrastructure priorities, improve coordination with TxDOT and local governments,_ and deliver real traffic relief and safety upgrades for residents.
9. **Stop Woke Indoctrination. Protect Parental Rights.**  
   Public schools should _teach academics_, not push divisive woke ideologies on race and gender. We will _stop indoctrination_ and restore parental rights so parents—not bureaucrats—control what their children learn and experience.
10. **Stop The Spread of Illegal Islamic Organizations in Texas.**  
    Texas must crack down on illegal Islamic organizations and networks that threaten public safety and American values. This includes _stricter enforcement, monitoring foreign funding, and preventing radical ideologies from taking root in our communities._
11. **Keep Our Elections Secure Every Cycle.**  
    Election integrity is non-negotiable. We will maintain _strong voter ID laws_, limit mail-in voting, ensure ballot security, and conduct regular audits to protect every legal vote and maintain public trust.
12. **DOGE Texas. Keep Texas Affordable.**  
    **[Pass open books laws to monitor state and local spending]**  
    We will bring DOGE-style efficiency to Texas government by _cutting waste and increasing accountability._ Passing open books laws will give Texans full transparency into state and local spending to keep taxes low and our state affordable.
